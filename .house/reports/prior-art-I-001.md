# Prior art: I-001 Submission radar with transparent scoring

- **Researcher:** coordinator (web search), 2026-11-21

## Closest existing
1. **EU Innovation Radar** (European Commission, JRC; live, 10,000+ innovations): identifies high-potential innovations in EU-funded projects, classifies market maturity (Exploring / Tech Ready / Business Ready / Market Ready) and market creation potential. Very close in purpose and even in name, but only for EU-funded R&D projects, scored by expert reviewers. https://innovation-radar.ec.europa.eu/methodology
2. **OECD OPSI Case Study Library** (since 2014, relaunched 2018): vetted library of public sector innovation cases, submitted via form, searchable by level of government, sector, stage; analysed on novelty, value creation, people-centricity, applicability. Close as a public-sector catalogue; no scoring or routing per case. https://oecd-opsi.org/submit-a-case-study/
3. **Ministerstwo Cyfryzacji: Baza innowacji publicznych / Mapa innowacji** (2022): shows the use of new technology in Polish local governments. Same organizer family as HackNation, so the jury may know it. https://www.gov.pl/web/ai/baza-innowacji-publicznych , https://www.gov.pl/web/cyfryzacja/mapa-innowacji
4. **Kuratoria: bazy innowacji pedagogicznych** (e.g. Kuratorium Warszawa): lists of school innovations as PDFs, no comparison or routing. https://kuratorium.waw.pl/pl/rodzice-i-uczniowie/eksperymenty-i-innowacj/baza-innowacji/

## What is genuinely different
Covers the whole chain the brief asks for (submit or detect → card → transparent score → similar → pathway and recipients → metrics) for schools, universities and local governments, including non-technological innovations, with an explanation per criterion instead of an expert-only verdict.

## Market signal
Strong: the EU, the OECD and the Polish ministry all run catalogues; none routes local, non-R&D innovations onward. The brief itself states the gap.

## Risk for the rubric
High novelty risk on "Pomysł i oryginalność": a juror who knows EU Innovation Radar or OPSI will say "this exists". Defence: cite them, reuse their maturity scale and OPSI's four dimensions as our transparent rubric ("built on standards"), and show what they lack: local, non-R&D scope and the step after identification.
