# Prior art: I-006 Radar funnel (database ingest pipeline)

- **Researcher:** coordinator, 2026-11-21 (round 1 web research reused; items marked * are from general knowledge, not re-checked today)

## Closest existing
1. **EU Innovation Radar** (EC/JRC, live, 10,000+ innovations): maturity classes and market-creation potential, but fed by expert reviews of EU-funded projects only. https://innovation-radar.ec.europa.eu/methodology
2. **OpenAIRE Research Graph*** (EU, live): harvests publications, datasets and projects from repositories (OAI-PMH) and links them; no innovation scoring or routing.
3. **Lens.org*** (Cambia, live): search over patents and scholarly works with links between them; a search tool, not a pipeline that judges and routes.
4. **ITONICS / StartUs Insights** (live): AI scouting funnels for corporates over startup data. https://www.itonics-innovation.com/blog/ai-technology-scouting

## What is genuinely different
A funnel over Polish public sources (UPRP, PAN libraries, Interreg, NCBR) that keeps only innovations born in public institutions, types them (including non-technological) and ends each one with a pathway and recipients, not a search result.

## Market signal
The brief lists exactly these databases; EU and OECD fund catalogues; corporate scouting is a paid market.

## Risk for the rubric
Medium novelty risk (a funnel is expected). Defence: the evidence-based maturity of I-007 and the transfer output of I-004/I-009 make the funnel distinctive. Execution risk: connectors (see `reports/data-access.md`).
