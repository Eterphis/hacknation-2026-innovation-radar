# Prior art: I-014 Advocate and reviewer agents

- **Researcher:** coordinator, 2026-11-21 (items marked * are from general knowledge, not re-checked today)

## Closest existing
1. **AI safety via debate*** (Irving, Christiano, Amodei, 2018): two models argue, a judge decides; research proposal.
2. **Multi-agent debate for factuality*** (Du et al., 2023): several model instances debate to reduce errors; research.
3. **Peer review / "red team" practice***: standard in grant evaluation (e.g. two independent reviewers).

## What is genuinely different
Applied to innovation assessment for officials: the output shows the strongest case for and against, each with evidence links, and a verdict; it guards against the model overrating every idea.

## Market signal
Two independent reviewers are standard in public grant evaluation (NCBR, NCN*), so officials already trust this pattern.

## Risk for the rubric
Low novelty risk in this domain; medium cost and latency (two to three LLM passes per record). Can be added as one extra "reviewer" pass on top of I-011 instead of a full debate.
