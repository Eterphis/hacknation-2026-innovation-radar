# Prior art: I-009 Proven elsewhere, deployed here

- **Researcher:** coordinator, 2026-11-21 (items marked * are from general knowledge, not re-checked today)

## Closest existing
1. **Interreg Europe Policy Learning Platform: Good Practices database*** (live): curated good practices from Interreg projects, searchable by topic; manual curation, no fit ranking for a given institution.
2. **keep.eu** (Interact, live): database of Interreg projects, partners and calls with CSV export. https://keep.eu/faq/api-how-to-access-keep_eu-data-in-open-data-format/
3. **„Innowacje w samorządzie”** (FFW for MFiPR, 2025-2028): 30 tested social innovations with implementation instructions, up to 300k PLN per municipality. https://wartowiedziec.pl/polityka-spoleczna/81123-innowacje-w-samorzadach
4. **Kohesio** (EC, live): EU cohesion projects by region. https://kohesio.ec.europa.eu/pl/informacje

## What is genuinely different
Reads funded project results across regions automatically and answers "which of these fits our institution here", with a transfer passport and a list of local adopters; the existing databases stop at the project description.

## Market signal
Strong: the national programme pays for replication of 30 hand-picked innovations; Interreg's whole purpose is transfer between regions.

## Risk for the rubric
Low novelty risk. Fit with "hidden local ideas": these are often already known within EU programmes; defend as "known in a database, unknown where they are needed". Data risk low (CSV export works without a key).
