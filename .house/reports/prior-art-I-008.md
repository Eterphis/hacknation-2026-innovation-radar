# Prior art: I-008 Dormant university patents

- **Researcher:** coordinator, 2026-11-21 (items marked * are from general knowledge, not re-checked today)

## Closest existing
1. **Enterprise Europe Network Partnering Opportunities*** (EU, live): database of technology offers and requests, hand-written by EEN advisors.
2. **Technology offer pages of university tech-transfer centres (CTT)*** (e.g. Polish universities): static lists of patents for licensing.
3. **IN-PART Connect** (UK, live): university ↔ industry matching. https://in-part.com/connect/
4. **EPO OPS / Espacenet**: the raw patent data. https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/data/web-services/ops

## What is genuinely different
Finds the dormant ones automatically (patent with no linked deployment) instead of relying on a tech-transfer officer to list them, and proposes a pathway including deployment in a public institution, not only licensing to a company.

## Market signal
Polish universities run CTTs and publish offers; commercialisation of public research is a stated policy goal.

## Risk for the rubric
C1 risk: patents are technological only, while the brief stresses organisational, educational and social innovations; jury may see it as too narrow. Good as one lens inside I-006.
