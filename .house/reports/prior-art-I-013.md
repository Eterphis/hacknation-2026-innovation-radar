# Prior art: I-013 Continuous radar with a recommendation inbox

- **Researcher:** coordinator, 2026-11-21 (items marked * are from general knowledge, not re-checked today)

## Closest existing
1. **Google Scholar Alerts*** (live): e-mail when new papers match a query; no assessment, no routing.
2. **Enterprise Europe Network partnering alerts*** (live): technology offers sent to registered organisations by advisors.
3. **ITONICS** (live): continuous technology radar for corporates. https://www.itonics-innovation.com/blog/ai-technology-scouting

## What is genuinely different
Push to public institutions: each subscribed school or municipality gets a short weekly list of innovations from other public institutions that fit its profile, with reasons; accept/reject feedback tunes the matching.

## Market signal
Alerts are a proven habit; EEN runs a funded network doing this by hand.

## Risk for the rubric
Low novelty on alerts. Demo risk: needs subscribed institution profiles and a time dimension that is hard to show in 3 minutes; feedback learning will not show results within 24 h.
