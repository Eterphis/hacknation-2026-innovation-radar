# Prior art: I-012 Agent on an official's request

- **Researcher:** coordinator, 2026-11-21 (items marked * are from general knowledge, not re-checked today)

## Closest existing
1. **Perplexity*** (live): question in, cited answer out, general web.
2. **Valuer.ai** (live): a company states a need, gets matched startups. https://tech.eu/profiles/27244/copenhagen-based-valuer-ai-identifies-some-of-europes-medtech-startups-to-watch/
3. **IN-PART Connect** (live): companies post needs, universities respond. https://in-part.com/connect/
4. **I-010 „Zapytaj radar”** (round 2): the same entry without an agent that plans its own queries.

## What is genuinely different
The agent plans queries across the brief's public databases, runs every hit through the same scoring pipeline, and answers an official's concrete need with a shortlist of innovations from other public institutions plus the reasoning.

## Market signal
Valuer and IN-PART sell matchmaking; GovTech challenges exist in Poland.

## Risk for the rubric
Medium novelty risk ("chat with AI"). Strong usability and a natural demo opener; alone it is detection on demand, not the systematic detection the mentors asked for (D-002), so pair it with a background pipeline.
