# Prior art: I-003 Problem ↔ solution exchange

- **Researcher:** coordinator (web search), 2026-11-21

## Closest existing
1. **IN-PART Connect** (UK, since 2014, live): matchmaking of university research with industry, 250+ universities and 6,000+ companies (self-reported); algorithms plus curation by STEM experts; universities pay. Very close in mechanism, but academia ↔ industry only. https://in-part.com/connect/
2. **Apolitical** (London, 2017, live): peer platform for public servants, 500k+ users (self-reported), communities and shared articles, connecting people "tackling the same problems". No structured matching of problems to solutions. https://apolitical.co/pages/our-story
3. **Giełda testowania rozwiązań informatycznych dla samorządów** (gov.pl): local governments test IT solutions. https://www.gov.pl/web/popcwsparcie/gielda-testowania-rozwiazan-informatycznych-dla-samorzadow
4. **GovTech Inno Lab** (PARP / GovTech Polska): challenges from public institutions solved by companies. https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/content/article/83236:polskie-samorzady-otwarte-na-innowacje-trwa-nabor-do-programu-govtech-inno-lab

## What is genuinely different
Both sides are public institutions: a problem in one municipality or school is matched with a solution already working in another, plus duplicate-work alerts. IN-PART does this for research ↔ business; GovTech for public problem ↔ company.

## Market signal
IN-PART is a paying business; GovTech challenges run in Poland; the brief explicitly names "ograniczanie ryzyka dublowania prac" and "organizacja potrzebująca podobnego rozwiązania".

## Risk for the rubric
Medium novelty risk: GovTech is the organizer's own programme, so position this as complementary (public ↔ public). A two-sided exchange needs seeded data on both sides for the demo.
