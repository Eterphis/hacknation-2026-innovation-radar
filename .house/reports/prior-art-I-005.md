# Prior art: I-005 Five-minute interview + regional map

- **Researcher:** coordinator (web search), 2026-11-21

## Closest existing
1. **OECD OPSI submission form** (see I-001): long structured form, then manual vetting. https://oecd-opsi.org/submit-a-case-study/
2. **IdeaScale** (live, commercial): idea management with campaigns and voting, used by governments.
3. **Go Vocal (formerly CitizenLab)** (live): citizen participation platform for municipalities, idea collection and analysis.
4. **MC Mapa innowacji** (see I-001): map view of public innovation.

## What is genuinely different
Cuts the effort of reporting for a teacher or clerk to a short conversation that keeps asking until the card is complete, instead of a long form; the operator sees a live regional funnel.

## Market signal
Idea management tools are a mature market; low reporting from public institutions is the problem the brief describes.

## Risk for the rubric
High novelty risk: chat intake and dashboards are expected features, not an idea. Good as the input layer of another idea; weak alone on "Pomysł i oryginalność" and only partial on scenario steps c-e.
