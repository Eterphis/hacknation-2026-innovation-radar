# Prior art: I-004 Transfer passport

- **Researcher:** coordinator (web search), 2026-11-21

## Closest existing
1. **„Innowacje w samorządzie”** (FFW for MFiPR, 2025-2028, live): municipalities get up to 300k PLN to implement one of 30 tested social innovations, each with detailed implementation instructions. Closest: proves the "replication package" format works, but the 30 packages are hand-written by experts. https://wartowiedziec.pl/polityka-spoleczna/81123-innowacje-w-samorzadach
2. **OECD OPSI** case studies (see I-001): applicability is one of four analysis dimensions, but no per-case replication plan.
3. **EU Innovation Radar** (see I-001): says what an innovation needs next (e.g. investor readiness), not who should adopt it.
4. **IN-PART** (see I-003): matching, without an adoption plan.

## What is genuinely different
Turns any submitted innovation into a replication package automatically and ranks concrete target institutions by fit (type, size, region, stated needs) using public registers, so "co dzieje się z innowacją po jej zidentyfikowaniu" (brief §9) gets a concrete answer.

## Market signal
Strong in Poland: a funded national programme already pays municipalities to replicate tested innovations, but only 30 hand-picked ones; the bottleneck is producing packages and finding adopters.

## Risk for the rubric
Low to medium novelty risk; the jury is an innovation centre and will recognise the gap. Quality risk: generated cost and effort estimates may look invented; mark them as assumptions and cite sources (brief §5 asks for stated assumptions and limits).
