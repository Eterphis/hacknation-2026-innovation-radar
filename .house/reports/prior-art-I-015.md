3. **Grant evaluation checklists*** (NCBR, EU programmes): criteria with yes/no and scores.

## What is genuinely different
Scores come from rules and thresholds with a traffic light per criterion and a link to the evidence; the LLM only summarises and translates into official language. Most predictable and cheapest option.
